# Kaya logo kit (concept 06)

Four Parang-curved blades turning around a Kawung centre, with a clay diamond (the celengan) in the middle.
"Four movements, one calm centre."

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#1F3D2B` | The mark on light backgrounds, app icon background |
| Cream | `#F4EEE2` | The mark on dark backgrounds |
| Clay | `#C2643A` | The diamond on light backgrounds |
| Clay light | `#E07A45` | The diamond on dark backgrounds |

Wordmark: "Kaya" in Source Serif 4 SemiBold, letter spacing 0.01em, already turned into outlines (no font needed).

## Rules

- Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width.
- Below 48px, use the simple version.
- Don't recolour the blades in clay, rotate the mark, add a shadow, or put it inside a circle or coin.
- The diamond is the only clay in the mark.
