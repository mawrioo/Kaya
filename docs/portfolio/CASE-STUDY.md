# Kaya: seeing everyday money clearly

Money can be spread across several apps: one bank for salary, another for daily spending, another for savings, and an investment account. Each shows a balance, but understanding the whole picture still takes work.

Kaya brings those pieces into one calm view. It tracks Rupiah across BCA, Bank Jago, Superbank and Pluang, with spending categories, budgets and savings goals. A quick entry such as `nasi goreng 25rb BCA` makes a small daily expense easier to record.

The tricky part is keeping the numbers honest. Moving money between your own wallets should not look like new income or spending. Kaya treats transfers separately and checks imported rows for duplicates before saving them.

The app uses Next.js and TypeScript, with a Hono backend and SQLite. Warm colours, clear amounts and plain language keep checking money approachable. It runs locally for one owner: simple data ownership, with manual entry and backups instead of automatic bank sync.
